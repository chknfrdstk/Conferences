- **Content:** "The cloud is just someone else's computer but your data is your own." Justin Campbell discusses common misconceptions about today's Internet that leave people unknowingly giving up their right to privacy — like Third Party Doctrine and social media — how to protect data at home with self-hosted infrastructure using ZFS and Samba, and how to keep access to your things secure even when crossing borders. He works in cybersecurity and travels with his devices, through countless border crossings, airport inspections, and insecure work environments, and demonstrates the self-hosted infrastructure that lets him travel knowing his network is secure, his data is intact, his privacy is maintained, and his downtime is minimal. Pretalx: Saturday 3:00 PM, Stadium. Speaker: Justin Campbell, VP of Engineering for a platform hardening tool; previously ran an IT consulting business, deployed Raspberry Pis on car batteries for hospitals in Kenya, and led an engineering migration of a large research university's tooling from Perl on Solaris to Python.
- **Most useful for:** self-hosters and privacy-minded travellers; anyone building a global backup/restore setup on ZFS and Samba.
