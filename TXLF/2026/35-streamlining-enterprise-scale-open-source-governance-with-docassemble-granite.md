- **Content:** At IBM there are over 9,000 open source engineers working in over 900 open source organizations. The people who review all open source contributions for compliance have legal oversight and must document everything. Until recently, engineers had to fill out word-processing templates and the compliance team reviewed each document line by line for every release — painful. This talk introduces the new pipeline for IBM open source compliance review: docassemble.org, an open source tool that guides engineers through questions to generate the needed documents, and Granite, a local open source AI model, to help compile, categorize, and streamline the feedback loop for the compliance team, so they can focus on content and legal questions instead of process. It tells the story of how they got there, what they learned, and includes simple code to help get this off the ground for your own processes.
