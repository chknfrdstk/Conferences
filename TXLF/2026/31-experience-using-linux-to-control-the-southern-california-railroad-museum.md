- **Content:** The Southern California Railroad Museum is one of the biggest transportation museums in the United States, and one major feature is its Signal Garden, where guests press a button and see signals work. Behind the scenes is a Linux Raspberry Pi controller. This talk discusses how the 100-year-old railroad signals are interfaced with the Pi and the unique challenges that had to be overcome — for example, the "Wig-Wag" signals use two huge electromagnets continually turned on and off, and turning off a big coil produces a huge EMP that will fry unprotected machinery — plus dealing with systems over 100 years old for which the documentation was lost sometime in the 1930s. And the time he started to upgrade the computer, opened the cabinet, saw a black widow making a nest on the main logic board, slammed the door shut, and got some spray to debug the computer.
