## Caveats

- Upload dates are inferred from YouTube relative ages ("3 days ago" = November 7, 2026; "2 days ago" = November 8, 2026). Exact clock times are not shown by YouTube and are not claimed here.
- View counts are as observed November 10, 2026 and change constantly.
- Two Friday upload titles appear truncated (RamaLama ends "audio p"; BPF-LSM ends "audio proc").
- Kory Heard's video is titled "FreeBSD" on YouTube but the slide/pretalx say 5BSD — apparent uploader error.
- The SELinux recording is 28:37 vs a 1h20m slot and may be partial.
- November 12 update: the YouTube descriptions and transcripts for talks 27–40 could not be verified (YouTube rate-limited the checks), so those files say "not verified" rather than "none". Three of them (Agentic Infrastructure, Effective HA, Don't Mess With My Data) have no pretalx abstract and are deliberately thin.
- Correction (November 12): pretalx lists Thomas Cameron alone for the OKD talk (#32); Neal Gompa presented a different, simultaneous talk and did not co-present OKD.
