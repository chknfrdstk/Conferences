- **Content:** IT is full of late nights, urgent tickets, and systems that always seem to fail right before a holiday weekend, and over time that pace catches up with you. Eric Hendricks shares what burnout really looks like from someone who has lived through it and how he found his way back to a healthier balance: the patterns that lead there, from over-commitment and constant context switching to the pressure to "always be learning", then practical ways to protect your energy while still loving the work — setting better boundaries, automating what drains you, and finding community outside the console.
